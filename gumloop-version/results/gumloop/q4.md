3. CRITERIA ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

Criterion 1: TECHNOLOGICAL UNCERTAINTY
─────────────────────────────────────────
Status: ❌ NOT MET

Justification:
Technological uncertainty exists when "it is unknown (or uncertain) whether a 
given result or objective can be achieved, due to an insufficiency in the 
available scientific or technological knowledge."

Your project faced no such uncertainty:

• FastAPI is a mature, well-documented framework with extensive examples
• JWT authentication is a standard, published security pattern (RFC 7519)
• Redis rate limiting is a documented, off-the-shelf pattern
• AWS ECS auto-scaling is a fully documented AWS feature
• CRUD operations are textbook API patterns

You knew at the outset how to build this. The question was not "Can this be done?"
but "How quickly can we implement it?" — an engineering schedule question, not 
a research question.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(Technological Uncertainty section, p. 3)
"It is needed when it is unknown (or uncertain) whether a given result or 
objective can be achieved, due to an insufficiency in the available scientific 
or technological knowledge."


Criterion 2: SYSTEMATIC INVESTIGATION
─────────────────────────────────────────
Status: ❌ NOT MET

Justification:
The CRA distinguishes between "a systematic approach to carrying out work" and 
"a systematic investigation or search" required for SR&ED.

Software development that follows architectural patterns is systematic. It may 
involve code review, testing, and iterative deployment — all methodical. But 
this is engineering process discipline, not SR&ED investigation.

A systematic investigation in SR&ED means:
• Generating hypotheses about unknown outcomes
• Testing hypotheses through experiment or analysis
• Evolving ideas based on results
• Documenting evidence of uncertainty resolution

Your project followed a known specification ("build a REST API for the mobile 
app"). You executed against that spec. There was no uncertainty to investigate.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(The "How" Requirement section, p. 4)
"It is important to distinguish between a systematic approach to carrying out 
work and the approach that is a systematic investigation or search called for 
in the definition of SR&ED."


Criterion 3: TECHNOLOGICAL ADVANCEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────
Status: ❌ NOT MET

Justification:
The CRA requires advancement of "the understanding of science or technology" — 
conceptual, generalizable knowledge, not business-specific implementations.

Your API:
• Did not advance the understanding of REST architecture (REST is from 2000)
• Did not advance the understanding of JWT (RFC 7519, 2015)
• Did not advance the understanding of rate limiting
• Did not contribute to FastAPI, Redis, or AWS knowledge
• Generated zero new conceptual knowledge

The API is a business application. It solves your company's problem using 
existing solutions. The CRA explicitly states:

"Acquiring available knowledge or know-how, for example, through training, 
on-the-job learning, hiring expert employees or consultants, or by purchasing 
proprietary knowledge does not meet the requirement of attempting to achieve 
technological advancement."

Your team acquired (through skill and experience) existing knowledge and applied 
it. That is not SR&ED.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(The "Why" Requirement section, p. 3; Experimental Development section, p. 7)

4. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE (CRA GUIDELINES)
================================================================================

EXCLUSION: Commercial Production and Business Application Development

The CRA's definition explicitly excludes:
"(i) the commercial production of a new or improved material, device or product 
or the commercial use of a new or improved process"

Your REST API is a commercial application. It is new to your company, but it 
uses processes (REST, JWT, rate limiting, cloud deployment) that are not new 
to the field. The CRA treats this as business application development, which is 
not SR&ED.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(Definition of SR&ED, paragraph (i), p. 2)

KEY DISTINCTION: What is NOT SR&ED

The guidelines illustrate this with examples:

"Work undertaken for the commercial production of a new or improved material, 
device or product, or the commercial use of a new or improved process are NOT 
eligible for SR&ED."

Building a REST API for internal business use (exposing customer data to a mobile 
app) is the commercial use of well-known processes (REST, authentication, 
deployment) to create a business product (the API). This is explicitly excluded.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(Categories of Ineligible Work, Excluded Work section, p. 10)

PURPOSE TEST: Why Was the Work Done?

The CRA asks: "Was it carried out specifically to perform the scientific 
research or experimental development? Or was it done for some other reason?"

Your API was built to:
✗ Enable the mobile app to access customer data
✗ Meet a business timeline (6 weeks)
✗ Serve a production use case

None of these are SR&ED purposes. They are normal business operations.

Citation: "Guidelines on the eligibility of work for scientific research and 
experimental development (SR&ED) tax incentives - Canada.ca.pdf" 
(Distinguishing between Support and Excluded Work, p. 10)

================================================================================
5. GAPS AND RISKS
================================================================================

Why This Claim Would Be Rejected Immediately:

1. **Explicit Exclusion Category**: Software development for business applications 
   falls squarely into the "commercial production of a new product" exclusion. 
   The CRA does not view building software for your company as R&D.

2. **No Uncertainty at Project Start**: You knew how to build this. FastAPI has 
   tutorials. AWS ECS has documentation. JWT is standardized. There was no 
   research question.

3. **Well-Established Patterns**: Your own description says you followed 
   "well-established architectural patterns." This disqualifies the work under 
   the uncertainty criterion.

4. **No Knowledge Contribution**: The API generates zero publications, no new 
   techniques, no generalizable insights. It is a business tool.

5. **Audit Risk**: The CRA routinely audits software development claims. Claiming 
   a standard REST API as SR&ED would be flagged immediately and could invite 
   scrutiny of your entire claim.

6. **Three Engineers, Six Weeks**: This timeline is typical for engineering 
   project work, not research. If the work were truly SR&ED (investigating 
   unknown technical challenges), the timeline would be unpredictable; you would 
   not scope it at "6 weeks and 3 engineers."

================================================================================
6. RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================

This project should NOT be claimed as SR&ED. Do not include it in your filing.

Including it would:
✗ Violate the explicit exclusion for "commercial production of a new product"
✗ Risk audit scrutiny of your entire SR&ED claim
✗ Weaken credibility if other projects are audited

WHY SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT IS RARELY SR&ED:

The CRA acknowledges that software development CAN be SR&ED, but only when:
• It addresses a *specific scientific or technological uncertainty*
• That uncertainty cannot be resolved by applying known methods
• The work generates new knowledge about how to resolve that uncertainty

Examples of software that MIGHT qualify (with strong evidence):
- "We investigated whether algorithm X could optimize database query planning 
   for edge cases, and designed experiments to test novel heuristics."
- "We studied whether cryptographic protocol Y could prevent timing attacks 
   in the presence of speculative execution, and implemented novel countermeasures."
- "We experimented with new compiler optimization techniques for SIMD-intensive 
   code."

Your REST API is NOT in this category. It is standard application development.

NEXT STEPS

2. **Focus on your GNN and distributed tracing projects** (if the latter is

4. **Software that could be SR&ED** requires advance documentation:
   - Problem statement: What is uncertain about solving this?
   - Hypothesis: What approach might work?
   - Experiment design: How will we test the hypothesis?
   - Evidence: What unexpected results emerged? What did we learn?

established processes. It does not meet SR&ED criteria.
